# Math Adventure — Expanded

The Math section now includes:

- Counting
- Addition
- Subtraction
- Simple division taught through equal sharing
- Greater than (>)
- Less than (<)
- Equal to (=)
- Shapes

To keep the lesson suitable for a kindergarten learner, the app randomly selects
6 Math activities from the larger question bank each time Math Adventure starts.

Examples:
- 5 - 2 = ?
- 4 cookies shared equally by 2 children = 2 each
- 5 > 3
- 2 < 3
- 3 = 3
