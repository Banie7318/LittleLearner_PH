# Filipino Reading — Expanded

The Filipino Reading section now follows a more gradual progression:

## Antas 1 — Patinig
A, E, I, O, U

## Antas 2 — BA Family
BA, BE, BI, BO, BU

## Antas 3 — More Pantig Families
MA, ME, MI, MO, MU
PA, PE, PI, PO, PU
SA, SE, SI, SO, SU
KA, KE, KI, KO, KU
LA, LE, LI, LO, LU
NA, NE, NI, NO, NU
TA, TE, TI, TO, TU

## Antas 4 — Pagbuo ng Salita
BA + TA = BATA
BO + LA = BOLA
MA + TA = MATA
PU + SA = PUSA
IS + DA = ISDA
NA + NAY = NANAY
TA + TAY = TATAY

## Antas 5 — Maikling Pagbasa
Simple words and short sentences.

The app randomly selects 7 Filipino activities per session so practice stays short
and varied for a kindergarten learner.
